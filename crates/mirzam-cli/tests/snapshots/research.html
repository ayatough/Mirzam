Background

One grating, two things to measure

  • A grating's reflected wavelength moves with strain and with temperature, and one reading cannot tell you which moved it[1]
  • Over our 120 m baseline the diurnal swing is worth more than the signal we are trying to see
  • So the array is only as good as the temperature model behind it — which is the part nobody publishes

A strain number without a temperature number is an opinion.

Do not derive the wavelength shift here; slide 5 has it.

Three ways people compensate, and what each costs

Approach Extra hardware Holds over Our verdict
Reference grating, unstrained One channel per zone Slow drift Channel budget too tight[2]
Athermal packaging Custom mounts Wide range Cannot retrofit the installed array[3]
Dual-wavelength matrix None Calibrated range Chosen — arithmetic, not parts[1]

See [2] for a survey of the field; this table is only the part that bears on a retrofit.

Method

Two wavelengths, one matrix

  • Each sensing point carries two gratings with different photo-elastic and thermo-optic coefficients
  • Their shifts are a linear system in (ε,ΔT), so inverting a 2×2 matrix separates them[1]
  • The matrix is fitted once per installation, in an oven, over the range the site actually sees

(Δλ1Δλ2)=(Kε1KT1Kε2KT2)(εΔT)

ε=KT2Δλ1−KT1Δλ2Kε1KT2−Kε2KT1

The denominator is the conditioning question someone always asks. Answer: 0.02 in our coefficient set, and that is slide 7.

Results

Apparent strain over a day, before and after

  • Peak apparent strain falls from 38 µε to 4 µε
  • What is left tracks the rate of temperature change, not its value — a lag, not a scale error
  • Across all 16 gratings the worst channel is 6 µε

The residual shape is the interesting part. Do not claim it as noise.

Discussion

Where it still fails

  • Outside the calibrated range the matrix is an extrapolation, and the coefficients are not linear there — the same limit the survey reports[2]
  • Fast transients beat the lag: a cold front moving over the span leaves the two gratings at different temperatures, and the model assumes they are not
  • Published drift budgets put a retrofit like ours near the floor of what is useful[4], which is an argument for packaging on the next array[3] rather than for a better fit on this one

If asked about the transient case: we have four events on record and none of them are in this dataset.
