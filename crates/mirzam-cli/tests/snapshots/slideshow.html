Slides on a loop

This deck is turning its own pages — autoplay: 6s loop in the frontmatter. A pauses it, H shows the controls it hides.

Everything on a slide plays on arrival; the loop only turns pages.

One photograph, one line

A full-bleed image and a caption that arrives on its own beat: an exhibition loop, a screensaver, a slideshow — the same file either way.

Captions on their own clock

Each line lands a beat after the one before — a delay= on its entrance.

Nothing waits for the page to turn; the loop keeps one clock.

Click steps would hold a slide one interval per step; a loop of photos wants everything on arrival instead.

The recipe

The whole file is Markdown

---
autoplay: 6s loop
---

::: pane hero {.bleed bg=photo.jpg dim=0.4 scrim=bottom}
## A caption that arrives on its own beat {.telop}

[And a line that follows it, unprompted.]{.sub}
:::

```anim
[enter] .telop : words blur-in 500ms stagger=60ms
[enter] .sub   : fade-in 400ms delay=900ms
```

One photo per slide, one anim block per caption. That is the entire pattern.
